**Dataset Overview**

GH2025 is a greenhouse mesocosm experiment comprising 450 lysimeter pots designed to explore soil–feedstock interactions in enhanced rock weathering (ERW) for carbon dioxide removal (CDR). This repository holds the characterisation data for the 29 natural, agricultural and engineered technosol soils and 19 feedstocks representing seven mineralogical and dissolution-kinetic categories, including industrial by-products, carbonates, carbonate–silicate mixes and a range of silicate feedstocks, as well as the experimental design, and the analytical methods, as published in the paper and its Supplementary Material.

The experimental design prioritises breadth over replication, using one pot per soil–feedstock combination and three soil-only controls per soil to maximise parameter-space coverage. The resulting dataset will support machine-learning approaches to identify the soil and feedstock properties governing alkalinity export and CDR, and to predict favourable combinations for scalable deployment.

**Repository Contents**

This repository contains one data workbook comprising the main-text and Supplementary Tables associated with the GH2025 methods paper. The workbook includes soil characterisation data, feedstock geochemical, mineralogical and physicochemical characterisation data, the full GH2025 experimental design and soil–feedstock treatment matrix, and analytical methods and associated methodological information reported in the manuscript and Supplementary Material.

Individual worksheets correspond to the tables cited in the manuscript and Supplementary Material. Table titles, units, abbreviations, analytical qualifiers and table-specific notes are provided within the relevant worksheets.
